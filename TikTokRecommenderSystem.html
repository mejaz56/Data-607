3) Reverse Engineering

TikTok’s Recommender System Workflow Reverse Engineering


” Each of these factors can play a larger or smaller role in what’s recommended and how content is ranked. How important each factor is can change over time.• The factors that play a larger role do the best job of showing us the types of content you might enjoy.

Each recommender system selects from a large collection of eligible content and ranks them based on the system’s prediction of how likely you’ll be interested in each one. In certain recommender systems, these predictions also consider interactions from others who appear to have similar interests. For example:

•If User A likes videos 1, 2, and 3;

•User B likes videos 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5;

•then the recommender system may predict User A will also like videos 4 and 5.

Since everyone has different preferences and signals, recommendation feeds like For You, Following, Friends, and LIVE remain unique to each person, even if some occasionally see the same content.

[from: TikTok’s support page]

Diversifying Recommendations:

Another point worth mentioning is that not only do existing user get shown content based on their user interaction, content information, and user information, TikTok also diversifies what they promote to the viewer. On their website, it is stated as follows:

*
“*****Our goal is to show you relevant content while also helping you discover new interests. We aim to diversify your feeds by introducing you to new creators and content so you can explore different perspectives and ideas. That’s why you may come across content in your For You and LIVE feeds that doesn’t appear to be relevant to your expressed interests. We also typically don’t recommend content you’ve already seen.”

4) Further Recommendation to make TikTok better:

I think the very thing that makes TikTok a unique and strong streaming platform, makes for it’s weakness. What I mean by that is, constant loop keep pushing user into a corner or narrow subculture or echo chamber.

They can provide a better refresh option, as right now their refresh option only temporarily pauses user preference.

I would actually like to have them give more control to the user of which type of recommender their user wants to follow, an active one or a passive one. Passive one would be what TikTok is showing right now, quiet aggressively. Active would be focusing more on what the user hit like, favourites, or interacts with.
